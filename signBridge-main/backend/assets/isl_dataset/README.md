## 🎥 Recording Guidelines for ISL

1. **Camera Framing**: Waist-up to head height. Both hands must stay fully inside the camera frame at all times.
2. **Background & Lighting**: Plain wall (solid blue, grey, or neutral), front lighting without harsh shadows or backlights.
3. **Execution**:
   - Begin with hands in a relaxed, resting position at lower chest.
   - Perform the sign cleanly and deliberately.
   - Hold the final sign configuration for ~0.5 seconds.
   - Return hands to the resting position.
4. **Duration**: 2 to 3 seconds per sign.
5. **Video Format**: MP4 (H.264 / AAC) or WebM, 720p (1280x720) or 1080p (1920x1080) at 30 fps.
